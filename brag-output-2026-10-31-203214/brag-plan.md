# Brag Plan: Production Readiness Checklist (founders and CTOs)

## What is this app?
A free checklist of 115 things worth checking before launch, in 10 areas, each with a review question and a trade-off, plus an optional AI review of your own code.

## The angle
Confidence at launch. Small teams ship fast with no review board, so things get missed. This gives them the questions a review board would ask. Calm, no hype.

## Hook (0-3.6s)
The page's headline on an outlined card: "Know what your architecture is missing before go-live". Visible at frame 0.

## Key moments
- "Small teams ship fast. Things get missed." (the worry, no product claim)
- The real Retries concern: review question and trade-off verbatim, then "Deferred" chosen.
- Three reveals: 115 things in 10 areas / mark each one and see where you stand / optional AI review that says "cannot determine" instead of guessing, you make the final call.

## Outro / punchline
"Free. Nothing to install." + Production Readiness Checklist + link.

## User flow worth showing
Open a concern -> read the question -> mark it decided / deferred / missing / not applicable.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: calm, professional, quiet confidence
- Interpretation: five scenes, long holds, soft fades, no jokes, plain language, no technical internals.

## Format: square - 1080x1080
## Duration: 22s

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Accent: #0B6E78 (bg #DDF1F2)

- Strongest visual element: outlined cards with hard offset shadows

## Share copy (draft)
Launching soon and not sure what you missed? ...

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12, volume 0.30, fade out last 1.5s

- Audio-reactive treatment: none (restraint, calm tone)
- SFX posture: sparse: soft drops on the three reveals, one click on the choice, one soft bell on the outro
- Restraint rule: nothing loud, no voiceover
